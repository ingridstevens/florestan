Florestan.exe
=============

Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music link, and the song or whole playlist lands in Apple Music.

Your responsibility
- Florestan is not a tool for copyright infringement. Only download content you own or have the rights to
  download: your own uploads, recordings you made, or music released for free download by its rights holder.
  Downloading anything else may break YouTube's Terms of Service and copyright law.
- You alone are responsible for what you download.

What you need
- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 26 or later.
- Nothing else: Florestan brings its own download tools.

Installing
1. Drag Florestan.exe into the Applications folder.
2. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software, because it isn't from the
   App Store or a registered Apple developer. To open it anyway:
   - Double-click Florestan.exe, then click Done in the message (not Move to Trash),
   - open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway" next to Florestan.exe,
   - confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" once more. You only need to do this once.
3. Florestan lives in the menu bar (the ♪ icon). Copy a YouTube link and press ⌥⌘Y, or paste it into the panel.

The first time you download
- macOS asks whether Florestan may control Music. Click OK; that's how songs get into your library.
- If downloads fail with "bot check", a VPN or office network is being blocked by YouTube. Turn the VPN off.

Optional
- Eusebius (mood playlists) and offline suggestions need Apple Intelligence:
  System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.
